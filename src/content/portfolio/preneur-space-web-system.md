Preneur.space is an independent digital consultancy founded to help solopreneurs and small-to-medium-sized businesses build, maintain, and optimise their digital infrastructure.

The consultancy specialises in WordPress and WooCommerce performance, conversion rate optimisation, technical SEO, and Google Ads management. It focuses on helping business owners overcome technical bottlenecks, improve online store performance, and make better data-driven decisions without the overhead of traditional agency engagements.

Rather than offering generic website development, Preneur.space takes a business-oriented approach to technology. Its services connect technical engineering with commercial objectives, addressing issues such as website performance, checkout friction, plugin conflicts, search visibility, advertising efficiency, and conversion tracking.

### Business Model

Preneur.space operates through a flexible consulting model, offering focused one-time audits, in-depth strategic consultations, and ongoing technical maintenance and advertising management.

This allows businesses to access specialised expertise based on their immediate needs without committing to long-term retainers from the outset.

### Target Market

The consultancy primarily serves independent entrepreneurs, online store owners, and growing businesses across Europe and the United States through a fully remote service model.

### Leadership & Philosophy

Founded and led by Joni Ilman Pahmi, Preneur.space combines hands-on software engineering experience with digital marketing and business-oriented problem solving.

Its philosophy is built around a simple principle: technology should serve business objectives, not become an unnecessary operational burden.

**Business Focus:** WordPress, WooCommerce, Digital Infrastructure, SEO, Conversion Optimisation, and Google Ads.

**Operating Model:** Independent, founder-led consultancy.
